# Simple Homemade Chocolate Sauce

> Five pantry ingredients, about 15 minutes. Pours thin, thickens as it cools.

| Total | Yield | Serves |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 15 min | ~14 oz | 7 (2 oz each) |

## Ingredients

- ½ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 cup sugar
- ⅛ teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ cup cold water
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla

## Method

1. Before turning on the heat, whisk the cocoa and sugar together in the saucepan until no lumps remain.
2. Whisk in the salt and water. Bring to a boil over medium to medium-high heat.
3. Turn it down to a simmer and stir constantly for about 30 seconds, then take it off the heat.
4. Let it cool, then stir in the vanilla. Pour into a glass jar and refrigerate.

## Notes

- It will look very thin coming off the heat. Don't cook it longer, because it thickens as it cools.
- Keeps up to 2 weeks in the fridge. Warm it before serving if it's too stiff to pour.
- Doubles easily.
- Don't can it, since chocolate sauce is low-acid.
- Variations from readers (untested by the author): milk instead of water for a richer sauce, a little instant coffee or extra cocoa for a darker sauce.
