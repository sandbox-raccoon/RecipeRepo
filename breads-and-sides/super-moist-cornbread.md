# Super Moist Cornbread

> Butter, oil, and eggs — no box required.

| Prep | Bake | Total | Oven | Pan | Serves |
|------|------|-------|------|-----|--------|
| 10 min | 35–45 min | 45 min | 350°F | 9 × 13 in | 12–16 |

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup sugar
- 1½ tablespoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ cup (8 tbsp) butter, melted
- ½ cup oil
- 1¼ cups milk
- 3 large eggs
- *Honey and extra butter, for serving (optional)*

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9×13 inch pan.
2. Whisk the flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl.
3. In a separate medium bowl, stir together the melted butter, oil, milk, and eggs.
4. Pour the wet mixture into the dry and stir just until everything comes together.
5. Scrape the batter into the pan. Bake 35–45 minutes, until the top is golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean or with a few dry crumbs — no wet batter.
6. Let it rest in the pan 15–20 minutes before cutting into squares. Serve warm with butter and honey.

## Notes

- Doubles well — split between two 9×13 pans for a crowd.
- Glass, metal, or cast iron all work; glass browns the crust a little more.
- Stir-ins: ½ cup shredded cheddar, ¼ cup diced jalapeños, a cup of corn kernels, or crumbled cooked bacon.
- Keeps 3 days airtight at room temperature, or a week in the fridge.
